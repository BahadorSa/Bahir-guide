How are these indicators calculated?
  • Trend: Current price compared with the average of the last 50 points of the 90-day chart (each point is about 18 hours, so roughly a 37-day average).
  • Averages: If the 20-point average (about 15 days) is above the 50-point average it is a “golden cross”; if it is below, a “death cross”.
  • RSI 14: On the 30-day chart with points about 6 hours apart. Above 70 is overbought, below 30 is oversold.
  • These are approximate and not identical to TradingView indicators.

4Practical guide

For crypto: a position calculator with leverage, high-volume hours and key rules from reliable sources.

Know this first

In crypto futures with leverage, if price moves a little against you you get liquidated and lose that position’s margin. With 20x leverage, about a 5% move against you is enough.

2xLiquidated at about a 50% move
5xLiquidated at about a 20% move
10xLiquidated at about a 10% move
20xLiquidated at about a 5% move
50xLiquidated at about a 2% move

Numbers are approximate and ignore fees and maintenance margin. In Europe the regulator (ESMA) allows retail clients at most 2x leverage on crypto CFDs, and according to its review 74 to 89 percent of retail CFD accounts lose money.

Order types on an exchange

What share of trades must you win?

The better your risk/reward, the lower the win rate you need to break even. Formula: required win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + reward)

Psychology: your main enemy is you

FOMO (fear of missing out)Price has jumped and you want in fast. It is usually the worst entry; wait for a pullback.
Revenge tradingAfter a loss you want to win it back fast and make your size bigger. After 2 losses in a row, stop for the day.
OvertradingYou trade every small move. Quality matters, not quantity.
Daily loss limitSet a limit, e.g. 3 to 4 percent of the account per day. When you hit it, stop trading.
Moving the stop lossHope replaces the plan. Only move your stop toward profit, never further away.

Security and scams

  • Never give your recovery phrase (seed phrase) or private key to anyone. No real exchange or support team will ever ask for it.
  • Guaranteed profit means scam. Like “double your coins” or signal sellers who promise sure profits.
  • Pump and dump: They push up the price of a small coin, sell themselves, and the price crashes.
  • Look-alike address: Before sending, check the full wallet address; don’t copy it from your transaction history.
  • Don’t open unknown links or QR codes and install exchange apps only from the official store.
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) should be on, with a different password for every site.
  • If someone offers to “recover lost money”, it is probably a second scam.

Golden rules to start

  1. Spot first, futures later. You can’t get liquidated on spot. Practise for a few months with spot or a demo account.
  2. Risk only 1 to 2 percent of your balance per trade. After 10 losses in a row most of your account is still there.
  3. Size from the stop loss (calculator above), not from how much leverage allows.
  4. Low leverage (3 to 5x at most) and make sure the stop is well before the liquidation price.
  5. Never enter without a stop loss and don’t move it back after entry. Risk/reward at least 1 to 2.
  6. Check the funding. If you hold a position for days, the funding rate can become expensive.
  7. Altcoins are riskier. They have high volatility and low volume; start with Bitcoin and Ethereum.
  8. Wait during major US news (like CPI inflation and Federal Reserve rate decisions).
  9. Check the exchange. In Austria, look it up in the FMA Unternehmensdatenbank to see if it has a MiCA licence. Don’t keep money you aren’t trading on an exchange.
  10. Keep a journal: reason for entry, stop loss, take profit, result, and how you felt.
